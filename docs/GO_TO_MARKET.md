# Go To Market

## Best First Segment

- international students
- early-career analyst candidates
- OPT / H-1B-conscious job seekers
- candidates applying to risk, finance, analytics, strategy, and consultant roles

## Core Message

Instaply helps candidates find realistic jobs, tailor stronger materials, and move toward supported auto-apply through a private assistant workflow.

## Best Launch Motion

### Phase 1

- manual onboarding
- free tester accounts
- direct user interviews
- packet quality feedback loops

### Phase 2

- paid self-serve plans
- content-led growth
- MCP / assistant-native distribution

### Phase 3

- coaching and university partnerships
- international student market
- team / admin plans
