# Tester Program

## Goal

Let selected testers try Instaply early, verify that it works, and give useful feedback before broad launch.

## Recommended Rules

- create internal tester accounts
- give free credits
- mark tester accounts with feature flags
- keep tester flows separate from normal customers

## Suggested Tester Benefits

- free starter applications
- higher packet limits
- early access to supported auto-apply
- direct feedback channel

## Suggested Internal Flags

- `tester_mode=true`
- `free_application_credits`
- `free_packet_credits`
- `apply_beta_enabled`

## Best Early Testers

- close friends actively job hunting
- international students
- risk / finance / analytics candidates
- people willing to share detailed bug reports
