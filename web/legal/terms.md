## 2. The Service

Instaply is an agentic software platform that automates the job-application process on behalf of its users. The Service:

- Discovers job postings from public Applicant Tracking Systems (Greenhouse, Lever, SmartRecruiters, Workday, Ashby, iCIMS, and others);
- Fills application forms using information you provide (resume, profile, preferences);
- Submits those applications to employers on your behalf;
- Verifies submission via confirmation-email matching;
- Maintains a record of your applications in your account.

The Service is available through three surfaces: (a) the web application at `instaply.asion.ai`; (b) the Claude Desktop MCP integration; (c) the ChatGPT Connector integration.
