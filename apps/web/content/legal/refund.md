## 1. Free Tier — No charge, no refund

Every new verified account receives **3 free application credits** on signup. The Free Tier requires no payment and is not eligible for a refund (there is nothing to refund).

## 2. Paid Credits — Our refund promise

We want you to be satisfied. Our refund rules are simple and consumer-friendly:

### 2.1 Automatic credit return on failed applications
If an application does not reach a **confirmed** state — because the form could not be completed, the submission was rejected by the ATS, or we could not verify a confirmation email within 30 minutes — **no credit is consumed**. This is enforced at the database level; no action is required from you.

### 2.2 14-day first-purchase refund
If you are dissatisfied with your **first paid credit pack purchase**, you may request a full refund within **14 days** of the purchase date, provided that:

- You have consumed **5 or fewer** paid credits during that period
- You contact us at **hello@asion.ai** within the 14-day window
- You briefly explain why the Service did not meet your expectations (helps us improve)

Refunds under this clause are issued to your original payment method within **7 business days** of approval. Refunds are processed by our payment processor, **Razorpay Software Private Limited**.

### 2.3 Unused credits on subsequent purchases
After your first pack, remaining **unused** credits from a later purchase may be refunded on a pro-rata basis within **14 days** of that purchase at our discretion, less any promotional bonus credits already consumed. Consumed credits are non-refundable.

### 2.4 Service outage refunds
If the Service is materially unavailable for more than **48 consecutive hours** due to our fault (not a third-party outage), we will credit your account pro rata for the downtime upon request.

### 2.5 No refund for hiring outcome
We provide application delivery, not hiring outcomes. **Dissatisfaction with the number of interview callbacks, offers, or hiring decisions received is not a basis for a refund**, consistent with Section 18 ("No Guarantee of Employment Outcomes") of our Terms of Service.

## 3. Credits — how they work

- The minimum purchase is **USD 10** (10 credits in the Starter pack). Larger packs include bonus credits.
- Credits are **consumed only on confirmed application submissions** (verified via confirmation email).
- Credits from failed, skipped, or unverified submissions are **not** consumed.
- Credits **do not expire** as long as your account remains active.
- Credits are **non-transferable** between accounts and **not redeemable for cash** except under Section 2.2 or 2.3.
- Promotional / bonus credits (e.g. the 3 free signup credits, or bonus credits in the Plus/Pro packs) are not refundable for cash in any circumstance.

## 4. Chargebacks

Please contact us at **hello@asion.ai** before initiating a chargeback. We will work in good faith to resolve any dispute. Chargebacks initiated without contacting us first may result in account suspension and a block on future purchases.
